For many residents living in cities nowadays, public transports might be the only choice they travel from a place to another. Public transport, as the most convenial and economic approach to commute, it indeed has more benefit than personal vehicle.a That is the main reason why government pays high attention to improving public transport and invests a ample amount of money in establishing an advanced infrastructure system. Currently, a controversial topic has drawn people attention, which is urge passengers to take public transport for free. The novel notion might in some perspectives. However, in my opinion, it is unrealistic to make it entirely free, but reducing the charges might make sense and be more beneficial

First, taking public transport for free will impose a financial burden on the government. Establishing more roads and overpasses requires an ample amount of investment. Furthermore, due to the rising price of oil, the operating costs of this system have reached historic point. In the other hand, if people no longer pay for buses rides, bus companies might face bankruptcy. In order to ensure the continuous development of public transport system, we should take action in advanced to cope with the situation that government can not satisfies the require of fund. In the same time, the revenue from public transport is a part of overall budget of build the development of city traffic transport system. Therefore, all citizens need to share the responsibility to this. After all, they are the primary beneficiaries.

Second, the capacity of current public transport falls far short of the demand for commuting. As fundamental economic principles tell us, when demand significantly exceeds supply, society may face potential crisis. This holds true for the traffic system as well. We can imagine that if people get permission to take public transport for free, the numbers of passenger would be blooming. Therefore, buses and subway would be seriously crowded and both driver and passengers would face a nightmare, which leads to the drop of quantity of service. People may have longer lines. It is a certain negative impact on people's daily life, even overall system may involve in chaos, which distrubrute more serious crisis.

As a matter of fact, reducing budget might be the better solution. In many cities, the cheaper and more convenient payment has been verified to be a effective measure to deal with transport issue. Taking Beijing as an example, which is one of cities that has decreased the bus charge through promoting the use of IC card. In fact, there is a couple of cities all around the world has adopted the same methods, which further show this effectiveness.

In conclusion, there are more hidden defects lying beneath this free-of-charge proposal than its possible benefits. Whether is should be finally implemented depends largely on respective transport systems in different regions. Only when the traffic network is fully developed to the highest degree can those predictable risks and drawbacks be totally eliminated.
