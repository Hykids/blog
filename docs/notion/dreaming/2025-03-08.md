I had a dream the other day. In it, I found myself at a harbor, where a couple of white sailboats drifted gentle under a sunny, blue sky. A towering, black statue stood on the coast. Though my memory is vague, I recall its shape, which seems to blend a human form with that of a cow, or perhaps it resembled the famous statue entitled *Let Us Beat Swords into Plowshares*. I can not sure, as the memory is unclear. And the sky, Jesus ,I've never seen the sky so clear in reality. I stood by the window in my dream, watching the sun pours in through the glass. Then, as I woke up, I felt a sense of lost so deep it nearly brought me to tears. If such a place truly exist in the world, I must visit it.
